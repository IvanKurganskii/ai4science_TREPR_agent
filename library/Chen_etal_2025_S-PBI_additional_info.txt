Abstract: We prepared Bodipy (BDP)-perylenebisimide (PBI) donor-acceptor dyads/triad to study the spin orbit charge transfer intersystem
crossing (SOCT-ISC). For BDP-PBI-3, in which BDP was attached at the imide position of PBI, higher singlet oxygen quantum yield ( = 85%)
was observed than the bay-substituted derivative BDP-PBI-1 ( = 30%). Femtosecond transient absorption spectra indicate slow Förster
resonance energy transfer (FRET; 40.4 ps) and charge separation (CS; 1.55 ns) in BDP-PBI-3, while for BDP-PBI-1, CS takes 2.8 ps. For triad
BDP-PBI-2, ultrafast FRET (149 fs) and CS (4.7 ps) process were observed, the subsequent charge recombination (CR) takes 5.8 ns and long
lived 3PBI* (179.8 µs) state is populated. Nanosecond transient absorption spectra of BDP-PBI-3 show that the CR gives upper triplet excited
state (3BDP*) and subsequently, via a slow intramolecular triplet energy transfer (14.5 µs), the 3PBI* state is finally populated, indicating that
upper triplet state is involved in SOCT-ISC.

Previous studies on electron donor-acceptor dyads showing
photo-induced electron transfer (or charge separation, CS) have
shown that ISC can be induced via charge recombination
(CR).[3840] Two different mechanisms have been proposed for the
triplet state population in donor-acceptor system through CR, i.e.
radical pair intersystem crossing (RP-ISC) and spin orbit charge
transfer intersystem crossing (SOCT-ISC).[41,42] RP-ISC requires
very weak coupling between donor and acceptor, in other words,
the electron exchange energy (J) is extremely small, which is
usually obtained by using a long, rigid linker to connect the donor
with acceptor. This results in an extremely small J, i.e. the small
energy gap between charge transfer (1CT/3CT) states. The main

Triplet excited state plays fundamental role in photochemical
applications, such as photodynamic therapy,[15] photovoltaics,[6,7]
photocatalysis,[813] and triplet-triplet annihilation upconversion.[14,
15]
Triplet excited state of organic compounds is formed through
intersystem crossing (ISC) after photoexcitation. ISC is an
electron spin forbidden process,[16] the most typical approach to
enhance ISC in organic compounds is the heavy atom effect,
realized by attaching heavy atoms, such as Ir, Ru, Pt, I and Br etc.,
to a chromophore.[1722] Some alternative approaches without
invoking of heavy atom effect are available, include use of spin
convertor,[2326] exciton coupling,[2729] singlet fission,[3032]
interaction of a photo-excited chromophore with stable radical
etc.[3335] However, these conventional methods suffer from some

However, the ISC kinetics of RP-ISC is slow, and the dyads

Recently, some compact donor-acceptor systems with
orthogonal geometry are found to exhibit efficient triplet
population, via SOCT-ISC mechanism.[38] In such kind of electron
donor-acceptor molecular systems, CR is accompanied with orbit
angular momentum change of electron, which off-sets its spin
angular momentum change during ISC, thus ISC is enhanced in
such orthogonal dyads.[38] A few examples of SOCT-ISC systems
were reported, including Bodipy-anthracene,[4346] Bodipyphenothiazine,[47]
naphthalene-acridine,[48]
phenothiazineanthracene[49] and carbazole-perylenebisimide donor-acceptor
dyads.[50] However, there is still much room left for studying the
SOCT-ISC mechanism. For instance, although it was proposed
previously that an upper triplet state should be involved in SOCTISC, there is no experimental evidence for this postulate.
Unfortunately, it is difficult to directly observe the upper triplet
state, because the upper triplet states are usually short-lived and
the internal conversion to low lying localized excited (3LE) state is
fast. Moreover, although it was assumed that 1CT3LE is fast in
the dyads showing SOCT-ISC, it’s still an open question whether
or not other processes also play a role in SOCT-ISC. Finally,
experimental evidence for the sign and magnitude of the J value
of compact electron donor-acceptor dyads was rarely reported.
Herein we designed a series of Bodipy (BDP)perylenebisimide (PBI) donor-acceptor dyads and triads by
connecting the BDP electron donor at either the bay or imide
position of PBI, and via one or two phenyl linkers (BDP-PBI-1,
BDP-PBI-2 and BDP-PBI-3. Scheme 1). The phenyl spacers
were introduced as a bridge between BDP and PBI moieties to
achieve the weak coupling between donor-acceptor moieties. The
aim of this work is to study the detail mechanism of the SOCTISC in the BDP-PBI donor-acceptor systems. The photophysical
properties of the dyads and triads were studied with steady state

and time-resolved transient optical spectroscopic methods, as
well as time-resolved electron paramagnetic resonance (TREPR)
spectroscopy. Photoinduced CS and CR and efficient SOCT-ISC
were observed. Interestingly, upper triplet state was observed, as
well as an unusually slow intramolecular triplet-triplet-energytransfer (TTET).

Results and Discussion

PBI is highly fluorescent and possess intense visible light
absorption, but it lacks ISC ability. [51] Previously, some attempts
were made to access the triplet state of PBI through SOCT-ISC
approach by introducing strong donor phenothiazine moiety at the
bay position of PBI, but no triplet formation upon photoexcitation
was observed.[52] This may be due to large energy gap between
CT (1.88 eV) and triplet states (1.20 eV).[52] Keeping this in mind,
we devised donor-acceptor dyads/triads by attaching weak donor
(BDP) moiety to the electron deficient PBI acceptor, with aim to
achieve better matching of CT (ca. 2.1 eV) and triplet state
energies (ca. 1.63 eV for the BDP unit) which in turn results in the
efficient triplet formation.
The synthesis of the compounds are based on the routine
derivatization chemistry of PBI and BDP chromophores. The
introducing of BDP moiety at the bay and imide position of PBI
chromophore were realized by Suzuki-Miyaura crossing coupling
reactions (Scheme 1). Proper reference compounds were
prepared and used in spectral studies.

The position of the attachment of PBI moiety with BDP
chromophore was varied to study the effect of mutual
chromophore orientation on the photophysical properties,
especially SOCT-ISC efficiency. Two dyads (BDP-PBI-1 and
BDP-PBI-3) were constructed by linking BDP moiety to the PBI
acceptor at the bay position and imide position, respectively. A
triad (BDP-PBI-2) was also prepared by introducing two BDP
units at the bay region of PBI. BDP-PBI-1 and BDP-PBI-2 were
reported previously,[53] but the triplet state formation was not
studied.
The ground state geometries of the BDP-PBI dyads/triad are
optimized (Figure 1). For BDP-PBI-1 and BDP-PBI-2, the
dihedral angles between the PBI and the intervening phenyl
moiety of BDP is 60°and 63°respectively. On the other hand, the
dihedral angle between the intervening phenyl ring and the PBI
moiety in BDP-PBI-3 is 94°. Moreover, BDP-PBI-1 and BDP-PBI2 show the distorted PBI core, which is common feature observed
in bay-substituted PBI derivatives.[52,54,55] For BDP-PBI-1, BDPPBI-2 and BDP-PBI-3, the distortion angles of PBI core are ca.
9.3°, 13.2° and 0.4° respectively. Previously, we showed that
substitution of anthryl unit at bay position of PBI leads to twisting
of PBI core by 14°to 17°.[54] Whereas in case of BDP-PBI-3, PBI
chromophore exhibit almost a planar geometry which indicates
that the substitution of BDP at imide position of PBI has negligible
effect on electronic feature of PBI. The PBI core was also found
to be planar in previously studied imide substituted PBI-Cz
dyads.[50] The dihedral angle between the planes of BDP and PBI
in BDP-PBI dyads/triads was also determined (Figure S26). For
BDP-PBI-1, BDP-PBI-2 and BDP-PBI-3, the dihedral angles
between BDP and PBI planes are ca. 36.7°, 34.8° and 40.7°
respectively.

Conclusion
In summary, boron-dipyrromethene (BDP)-perylenebisimide (PBI)
donor-acceptor dyads and triad were prepared by attaching the
electron donor BDP at either the bay or the imide position of PBI
unit, to study the spin orbit charge recombination-induced

Photo-reduction of the dyad in the presence of
sacrificial electron donor was achieved and reversible generation
of the near IR-absorbing PBI radical anion formation was
observed. This study is useful to clarify the photophysical pathway
involved in SOCT-ISC, the related electron spin dynamics, and for
design of new heavy atom-free triplet photosensitizers.
